Electrostatic Discharge Safety Awareness for Electronics Assembly

Articulate Rise 360  |  Defense Electronics Manufacturing  |  Compliance Training

Overview

A 12-minute compliance module designed for new technicians entering ESD Protected Areas in defense electronics manufacturing environments. The course was developed and branded to align with Raytheon's visual identity so learners would experience it as a credible internal resource rather than generic off-the-shelf training.

Design Approach

The module follows a structured ADDIE framework across six lessons: establishing context and objectives, building conceptual understanding, introducing procedural knowledge, and assessing retention. Content was chunked using tabbed interactions to reduce cognitive load. A 5-step procedural sequence guides learners through EPA entry protocols with a defined 80% passing threshold tied directly to the learning objectives.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify conditions and sources that generate electrostatic discharge
  • Recognize the components and rules of an ESD Protected Area
  • Demonstrate proper grounding and PPE procedures before entering an EPA
  • Explain why latent ESD damage is a critical risk in defense electronics manufacturing

Aviation Ground Crew VR Training Suite

NetEdgeVR  |  New Hire Onboarding  |  Immersive Learning Environment

Overview

New aviation ground crew hires were entering the job having only experienced concept-based computer training — they understood procedures in theory but had never seen a real ramp environment before their first day. NetEdgeVR developed an immersive VR training suite to bridge that gap, giving learners a safe, realistic environment to acclimate to aircraft, ground equipment, and ramp operations before ever setting foot on an active airfield.

My Contribution

As part of a two-person art team, I designed and built the 3D models and environments across aircraft pushback, belt loader, tug vehicle, and aircraft marshaling modules. I owned the UI/UX graphical design across the application suite and participated directly in formative evaluation — conducting QA reviews to assess whether narration language, pacing, and tone were producing the intended learner response.

Design Approach

A self-paced walkaround mechanic allows learners to move through the environment at their own pace, processing audio narration and visual context simultaneously. Competency-based progression and customizable activity tracking allow organizations to monitor and report learner performance.

Tools Used

3ds Max, Substance Painter, Adobe Creative Suite, VR Hardware Integration

Offensive Line Curriculum Development & Performance Coaching

Northland Christian School Varsity Football  |  2014–2016

Overview

Given full responsibility for coaching the offensive line unit at a demanding private school program with consistent championship aspirations. My first sustained experience diagnosing performance gaps across individuals with different abilities, personalities, and learning styles — and developing them into a cohesive, high-performing unit.

Design Approach

Coaching an offensive line is fundamentally a curriculum problem — building shared mental models, synchronized execution, and the ability to adapt under pressure. My approach involved breaking down complex blocking schemes into teachable fundamentals, assessing individual development continuously, and adjusting instruction based on what was and wasn't translating to game performance.

Outcomes

The program reached the state semifinals twice and the state championship once during my tenure. The freshman I began developing in my first year went on to win a State Championship the year after I left — a result that reflects foundational instruction that produces durable performance, not just short-term compliance.

Core Competencies Demonstrated

  • Needs analysis and performance gap identification
  • Performance-based instruction and differentiated coaching
  • Formative assessment and iterative adjustment
  • Change management across diverse group dynamics
