Quick glossary

Words you'll see

Lead
Someone who might buy or sell. "Hot" means ready now, "warm" means within months, "cold" means a year or more out.
IABS notice
A short Texas form explaining how brokers work. Agents must share it the first time they talk with someone about a specific home.
Buyer agreement
Since 2026, Texas requires a signed agreement before an agent shows a buyer any home. It states the agent's pay, which is always negotiable.
Fair housing
Laws that forbid steering people or describing who "should" live somewhere. Agents describe homes, not neighbors.
CMA
A comparison of recent nearby sales that estimates what a home is worth. It is not an appraisal.
Option period
In Texas, a few days after signing when the buyer can back out for any reason by paying a small fee. It ends at 5 p.m. on the last day.
Earnest money
A good-faith deposit the buyer pays to the title company within 3 days of signing.
Title company
The neutral company that holds the money, checks ownership and runs the closing.
Net to seller
What the seller actually takes home after the loan payoff, fees and credits.
Intermediary
Texas has no "dual agency". If one brokerage represents both buyer and seller, both must agree in writing first.
