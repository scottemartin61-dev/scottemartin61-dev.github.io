# Sample leads for testing

Fictional inquiries to paste into `/realtor-pipeline:new-lead`, plus call notes for `/realtor-pipeline:qualify-lead`.

## 1. Portal inquiry (buyer, likely hot)

```
New Zillow lead
Name: Maria Alvarez
Email: maria.alvarez@example.com
Phone: (555) 010-2231
Property: 412 Birch Lane — $485,000 — 3 bd / 2 ba
Message: Hi, is this still available? We're pre-approved and our lease is up at the end of January. Could we see it this weekend?
```

Call notes afterward:

```
Talked 10 min. Not working with an agent. Pre-approved up to $510k with First Federal. Lease ends Jan 31, wants to close by then. Looking in Birchwood and Maple Heights. Needs 3 bed, garage. Wants to tour Saturday. Very responsive.
```
